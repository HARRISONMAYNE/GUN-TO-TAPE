Privacy notice

Gun to Tape is an independent search tool for UK road and cross country results. No race organiser or timing company runs it or is affiliated with it.

What we hold

For each result: the athlete's name, club, age category, finishing position and time, race number, and a link to the page on the organiser's own results site. We hold no addresses, dates of birth, email addresses or payment details for athletes.

Where it comes from

Only from results that organisers and timing companies have already published, collected within each site's published rules (its robots.txt) and at a gentle pace, or supplied to us directly by the organiser or timing company. Where a site asks not to be collected, or needs a login we have not been given, we do not collect it.

Why we hold it

Our legitimate interest is helping athletes, clubs, coaches and commentators find published results in one place. The results are already public. We make them easier to find and always link to the original.

Your rights

  • You can ask us to remove your results at any time, for any reason. Use the removal form. We reply within 30 days, usually much sooner.
  • After removal, your name goes on a block list so your results do not come back when the site refreshes.
  • You can ask what we hold about you and ask us to correct it. Send corrections to a time or position to the organiser too, because the original is theirs.
  • If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Cookies and tracking

This site sets no cookies and runs no advertising or analytics trackers.

Removal requests

We use the name and email address you give on the removal form only to deal with your request and reply to you. We delete them once the request is complete, apart from the name on the block list.

Remove my results

Give your name exactly as it appears in the results, and an email address for our reply. We will remove every result under that name and stop it coming back.
